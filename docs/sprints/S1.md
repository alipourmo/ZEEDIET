## S1-T1 built

- `database/migrations`: `users` (staff schema), `sessions` (database driver), `settings`, `audit_logs` (append-only, no updated_at).
- `app/Enums/StaffRole.php`: admin, secretary, operator, specialist.
- `app/Models/User.php`: explicit `$fillable`, `hasPermission()` reads config/permissions.php. `Setting`, `AuditLog` (refuses update and delete).
- `config/permissions.php`: the permission keys and the role map from spec section 5, plus `area.*` keys.
- `app/Providers/AuthServiceProvider.php`: one Gate per permission key.
- `app/Http/Middleware/EnsureAreaAccess.php`: alias `area:<name>`, registered in `bootstrap/app.php`.
- Factories: `UserFactory` with `role()` and `inactive()`.

## S1-T2 built

- Routes: `GET/POST /login`, `POST /logout`, `GET/POST /password/change` (auth), panel homes `/admin`, `/operator`, `/specialist` (auth + area).
- `LoginController` (Auth::attempt with is_active, RateLimiter 5/min per mobile+IP), `PasswordController`, `PanelController` (placeholder pages).
- `LoginRequest`, `ChangePasswordRequest` (min 10 characters, confirmed, different from current).
- Middleware in the `web` group: `EnsureStaffIsActive` (logs out deactivated users), `EnsurePasswordIsChanged` (forced change).
- `User::defaultPanelRoute()` chooses the panel by permission, not role name.
- `StaffSeeder` (one account per role, random printed passwords, placeholder mobiles); `DatabaseSeeder` calls it.
- Views: `auth/login`, `auth/change-password`, `panels/home`. Language: `lang/fa/auth.php`, `panels.php`, `seeders.php`, `validation.php`.

## S1-T5 built

- `App\Support\ClientAlertBadges`: allergy yes/no, contraindication keys and pregnancy status, computed in SQL. The free text is not loaded for badges.
- `components/alert-badges` (Blade): neutral, alert and info badges. Used on the client list (one extra column), and on the client file.
- `clients/_tabs`: Summary and Medical tabs. The Medical tab shows only with `medical.view`.
- Tests: `MedicalAlertsTest` (secretary sees badges and not the text, neutral state, medical tab per role, badges hidden without `medical.view_alerts`, one medical query per list page).
