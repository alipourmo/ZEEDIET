## Deferred from S3 (not dropped)

- **Override of a contraindication or a range error** by admin or specialist (spec 7.3 and 7.4). The MVP simplification applies: operators cannot override, and nothing in S3 offers an override path. Needs the override checkbox, the reason, and the `override:{client}:{device}` setting or parameters.override.
- **Walk-in sessions** (spec 7.3, admin and specialist only). Needs a route outside the operator area.
- **Cancelled and missed device sessions** in the UI. The enum and the package rule exist (S3-T1), but the operator page records only done sessions.
- **Specialist review and publish** of measurement sets (`pending_review` to `published`, `reviewed_by`, `published_at`, corrections with `supersedes_id`). Belongs to S4.
- **Measurement charts and latest values**, which read published, non-superseded sets only (the `current` scope exists).
